How SDi Works

Cost-aware · RBAC-grounded · MCP-tooled
1

Sits inside the SD app you already have

Zero new login. Uses your existing SD session for identity and RBAC scope — SDi only sees what you are allowed to see.

2

Intent router — 70% of turns answered without an LLM

Cheap local classifier routes each message: tips, data, news, education, or chit-chat. Only data synthesis hits an LLM.

3

BigQuery via the MCP tool you already built

RBAC-scoped SQL only. Pre-vetted templates against tod-prod.tod_data_warehouse (hevo_subscriptions, hevo_incidents, hevo_devices, hevo_organizations). No free-form SQL. No PII.

4

Three money moments

Revenue Recovery · finds delinquent high-value units. Upsell Whisper · spots tenants ripe for upgrades. Churn Save · flags drift before move-out.

5

Hard cost ceiling

Throttle (3/min · 50/day per user) · response cache (5 min) · cheap-model-first. Estimated < $0.10/user/month. Sells at $19/facility/month. ~200× margin.

6

Pricing SKU

Lite (free, stickiness) · Pro $19/facility/mo (money moments) · Enterprise $99/portfolio/mo. Even 15% attach = six-figure ARR on existing book.
